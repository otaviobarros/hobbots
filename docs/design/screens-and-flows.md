## Post-MVP (kept from the full-app design)

- **Mobile app:** a client of the hub on the same API, approved like any device.
- **More connection routes:** relay, local network with HTTPS, public HTTPS and a server install.

- **Routines:** chores on a schedule; returns to the sidebar when shipped.
- **Memory:** what each hob remembers, readable and editable; "About you" is shared.
- **Rooms:** group chats with several hobs; shown in the MVP only as a Later card.

## Handoff sheets

- **Component sheet:** tokens, type, every control and state listed in the [design specification](ui-ux.md#components).
- **Hob sprite sheet:** the nine sprite states with timing, seeded looks, size rules and how states map to real task status.

## End-to-end interaction checks

1. First launch: Welcome → Where Hobbots runs (run here) → Onboarding with a connected provider → first hob → Home.
2. Open a hob from Home; its page shows its chats. Open one, or start a new chat from the page.
3. Ask a hob for a long Task and watch the card show its steps. Ask for work that needs two more hobs; follow the Quest from the card, strip and drawer, answer an escalated question, and open the Quest tab and a Task's Workbench without leaving the lead's chat. Check that a party member shows "On <lead>'s quest" on Home. Pin the Quest and switch threads.
4. Approve once, for This Task (This Quest once a party joins) and always for this site; deny with a reason; confirm an expired request cannot be approved late.
5. Stop a reply versus Cancel a Quest; show their different scopes and outcomes.
6. Disconnect and restart at a controlled point; the reconnecting banner shows, queued messages send, durable history and waits are intact.
7. Retire a busy hob with each work option; its history remains inspectable.
8. Archive a thread, undo it, archive it again, find it by search, read it, and restore it; try to archive a thread while a reply runs.
9. Turn on Reduce motion; every animation stops.
10. Turn on remote access, open the hub address in a tailnet browser, Allow it on the hub, then Revoke it; the browser is refused at once.
11. Close the hub window during a Quest; the work continues and a connected device follows it live.
12. Open a finished Task's Workbench from its card: Summary shows first, the header reads History, a tab it did not use is dimmed and says Not used in this task, Browser reads Soon. Open Changes, Files and Terminal, switch Attempts, then go back to the same chat with the Quest still pinned.

## Explicitly excluded

Company fleets, team spaces, employee RBAC, mandatory approval for every action, recurring schedules, long-term memory, marketplace publishing and native mobile apps stay outside the MVP. Hobs never act on the files or apps of a connected device.
