# Design specification

Decided in [ADR 008](../decisions/008-design.md). This document records what the code must match. The [screen inventory](screens-and-flows.md) lists every screen and its states.

Hobbots is a modern AI chat app with a pixel-folklore character: small hobs that work at night in a lantern-lit workshop. Familiar interactions come first. The art, the hob animations and the names carry the personality; they never hide what a hob or an action actually does.

## Style tokens

The app has four themes, set with `data-theme` on the root element. Their values and rules are in [themes.md](themes.md) and [themes.json](themes.json). The app opens in Workshop Night, the night palette below, until a saved choice lands (#480). The table names today's uses; the redesign (#462) moves the screens onto the full set.

All text passes WCAG AA; the lowest pair is 5.6:1. Control borders are at least 3:1.

## Type

- **Atkinson Hyperlegible Next** for all reading text: chat, lists, buttons, fields. Body 15px.
- **Pixelify Sans** (ligatures off) only for headings, hob names and uppercase section labels, never below 14px.
- Fonts are bundled with the app; no network font loading.

## Shape and motion

- Pixel corners (a 4px clipped corner) on cards and buttons. Primary buttons have a 4px "press" edge and press down 2px.
- Focus is a 3px moonlight ring; inside clipped shapes it is drawn inset.
- Selection uses a fill (`#2c2a5a`), never a colored left edge. **No decorative left-edge accent borders** anywhere.
- Motion is stepped (pixel timing): hover a hob and it hops; working hobs bob; thinking shows blinking dots; streaming shows a moonlight caret; new messages, and the line that says how a reply ended (stopped, cut off, limited or failed), slide in; approve throws sparkles, deny shakes, forget poofs, sleep shows Zs. **Every animation stops with Reduce motion.**
- Each hob is a 14×14 pixel avatar generated from a seed (hat, gear, face, ears, beard, outfit, skin) plus one of six colors (Violet, Moss, Amber, Rose, Sky, Ember). The seed and color are saved with the hob, so the same hob looks the same on every device. The Hobbots mark, on the rail and the app icon, is the chief of the hobs: a 20×20 elder hob built from the same parts, in a gem crown with a lantern staff. On the rail the chief keeps still and its lantern speaks for the hobs: it looks around once when the app opens and on hover; while any hob works the lantern burns brighter with a slow flicker; while a request waits in the Inbox it raises a blazing amber lantern that pulses, shows a "!", links to the Inbox and says how many requests wait. With Reduce motion the colors, raised lantern and "!" stay and nothing moves. The needs-you mood follows the Inbox live: it shows as soon as a request arrives and ends once every request is decided; its link reads "Hobbots inbox, something needs you" or "Hobbots inbox, 3 things need you", counting stuck work too, and otherwise "Hobbots home". In a development build, `?chief=idle`, `?chief=working` or `?chief=needs-you` previews a mood. The sprite sheet defines nine states: idle, working, thinking, waiting, needs you, asleep, celebrating, failed, sending.

## Layout

- **Title strip**, 30px, with the window controls.
- **Rail**, 76px: the chief of the hobs, Home, Hobs, Inbox (needs-you count), Usage, and Settings at the bottom. Icons are 12×12 pixel art; the active item is moonlight on line.
- **Hob panel**, 256px: search over hobs, threads and messages, whose results replace the hob list while a search is typed; "Hobs" label; each hob row (sprite, name, role · live thread count, or "On Aldric's quest" linking to the Quest while it works on another hob's Quest, then "2 in the backpack" in lantern while items wait for it) opens into its threads, each with a ⋯ menu on hover and focus, with what it is waiting on above them: each backpack item's status square, what it is ("Quill's result for "Draft the landing copy""), since when and its state tag (Waiting, Delivered, Acknowledged, Reading, Stuck, then Consumed, Answered, Late or Dismissed for a minute once handled), opening its Task, with Retry and Dismiss once stuck, at most four with "N more", and routine progress as one line ("2 progress updates ride along the next turn"); "+ New thread"; "Archived · N" when the hob has archived threads; a "Rooms: the great hall" card tagged Later; "+ Summon a hob"; key hints. Collapses.
- **Chat**: header with sprite, name, thread title, role, a ⋯ menu with Archive thread, brain chip (the runtime's tile, runtime · model · effort, and health), which in a thread opens the brain picker: each runtime with its models, or why it cannot think now; the efforts the current model takes, as a segmented row; "Also make it <Hob>'s default"; "Back to <Hob>'s default" when the thread has its own brain. Where a reply's brain differs from the reply before it, the transcript shows a centered note, "Now thinking with Codex · <model>", and after a switch before the next reply, "The next reply thinks with …". A limited or cut-off reply offers "Continue with <runtime>" for each other ready runtime beside Continue from here, Edit hob and the right-panel toggle. My messages are right-aligned on a line-colored bubble (max 460px); a hob's messages show its 26px sprite and name in moonlight. A new thread shows "A fresh page" with idea chips. Above a hob's reply, its steps sit in a panel box with a line border: ✓ done in mint, a blinking ● running in lantern, ◷ waiting for approval in lantern, ✗ failed and ⊘ not allowed in rose (with the reason under it, saying whether the hob's permissions, you or the sandbox refused it; a Hobbots tool call the hub refused reads "Hobbots refused this: …" under its ✗), ■ did not finish in label, and a bold ? in lantern for a tool call cut off while it ran, with "Cut off while it ran, so it may or may not have taken effect. Hobbots won't run it again. Check it before you continue." under it and, when opened, when it started. A command step's label reads what the command does: it leaves out leading `VAR=value` assignments that only set how output looks or switch a config file off, such as `GIT_CONFIG_GLOBAL=/dev/null` or `NO_COLOR=1`, and keeps any other, cuts a path of four parts or more to its last two (`…/src/app.ts`), and when the whole command is one Git command it leaves out `-C`, since the step names that folder. A push, which the step names by remote and branch, and a Git command with `--git-dir` or `--work-tree`, which is decided on more than one folder, keep every folder option. A tool step opens to show the tool, the action in the Permissions tab's words and what it acted on, then its Output on deep with a line border, in monospace, with secrets already redacted. Short output shows whole. Long output shows its first lines, a dashed line saying "148.9 kB left out here", and its last lines, which open scrolled to the end where an error usually is; each part scrolls past 240px. Under it, "Open the full log (165 kB)" in lantern opens the whole redacted log in the browser, or "The full log could not be kept." in label. A finished reply keeps its tool steps and drops its thinking. A step waiting for approval has its approval card under the steps, ringed in lantern: the hob's sprite and "<Hob> asks permission", when it expires, then the action in the Permissions tab's words with the exact command or path, what it acts on, who asked, why now and the risk; Allow once (approve) and Deny (deny). A high-risk request (a force push or a push to the default branch) shows "High risk · once only" in rose under Risk and a check box, "I checked this action. Let <Hob> do it this once.", which enables Allow once. Deny opens an optional reason with Deny and Cancel and Escape closes it. Once decided the ring turns mint with a pop for Allowed, rose with a shake for Denied with the reason, or label for Expired, and the buttons give way to that line. Composer: a plain-text input on deep with a control border, Send in lantern, Stop in rose, hint line. It takes no Markdown and shows no formatting toolbar: what is typed or pasted is sent as is, with every line break, tab, space and blank line, and pasted rich text keeps only its text. A draft of only whitespace is not sent. It grows with its text to 160px, then scrolls, and the transcript keeps its last message in view while it grows. Enter sends, Shift+Enter adds a line, and Enter that ends an IME composition never sends. The input clears as soon as a message is sent and keeps focus after sending and when a reply ends; a refused send puts its text back. Each thread and fresh page keeps its own unsent draft, also across a reload, and Up on an empty input brings back the last message sent in the thread to edit. The hint reads "Enter sends · Shift+Enter adds a line · ↑ brings back your last message". Stop ends only the current reply; Cancel lives on the Quest. Under a request that started a Task, a Task card sits in the transcript (the Quest card once a party joins), ringed in control, lantern while something needs you, mint once done and rose once failed: the owner's sprite and "Aldric is on it" (then "Aldric finished this"), the Task's title, "1 of 2 done" with a mint progress line, the party's sprites with "Aldric's party: Quill and Ada" for a Quest, work about it that is stuck (below), and at most four rows (status square, owner sprite, Task or step title, the owner and what it waits on or last reported, and a status tag), ordered needs you, failed, blocked, running, waiting, with done rows folded into a count, more rows counted, and Show all. A Task with steps shows only its current step and "step 3 of 7". While work runs, a strip under the chat header shows "Aldric's quest" with the lead's and the party's sprites and counts ("1 needs you", "1 running"), never rows, and it leaves once the work ends. A row, the strip or Show all opens a drawer over the end of the chat, 420px with a control edge: Back to the Task above, the Task's status and owner, "Aldric gave it" with the brief, its milestones, the Tasks under it, its checkpoints (time, kind tag and what was reported), its questions with their answers (a question waiting for you shows "Ada asks you. Your answer goes straight back to Ada." in lantern with the same answer box as the chat's card, and one waiting on a lead reads "Waiting for Aldric to answer."), its output and, under Handed to it, what its lead handed to it and what each Task it comes after or was handed from returned ("Quill's result: …", with all its artifacts for a Task it comes after and only the ones handed on otherwise), then "Tell Aldric about this task", which sends your note to the lead's chat as "About "<Task>": <note>". A note is never an answer: while a question on the Task waits for you, the box is named "Note to Aldric, not an answer" and the line under it reads "This goes to Aldric as a note. To answer Ada, use the answer box above." The open Task is in the address (`?task=`), its title takes focus, and Escape or Close shuts it. The chat itself gains only the moments that matter: a question that reached you ("Ada asks you", ringed in lantern, with your answer box, then "You answered: …" in mint), a quiet line when the lead answered a member for you, and the lead's wrap-up. Stuck work is a blocker: work a hob was handed that ran out of tries, every turn ending early, or whose hob retired (IN-007). It shows on the Task card it is about, which it rings in rose, and in Inbox › Needs you, as a row that opens the same card beside the list. The card is ringed in rose: the hob's sprite and "<Hob> could not carry on", what the work was ("Reading the result of "Outline the launch""), a Stuck tag in rose, why ("It was tried 3 times, and every turn ended early." with how the last try ended, such as "You stopped the last try.", then "Details:" with the error that try recorded in monospace when it recorded one, or "Quill retired, so nobody can take it."), the next action in lantern ("Next: retry it" or "Next: dismiss it"), then Retry (primary when it is the next action, absent once the hob retired), Reassign (disabled, with "Reassign arrives once a Task can change owners."), Dismiss, and Open in the chat outside the chat. Retry starts the work again with as many tries as before, even where you had stopped it; Dismiss settles it; each choice is kept on the item and the card leaves.
- **Right panel**, 340px, tabs **Quest**, **Inbox**, **Workshop**. ## Components

The component sheet defines: buttons (primary, approve, secondary, deny, quiet, disabled, icon, chip), switch, segmented control, inputs, status tags, Workbench view labels, delivery steps (Sent, Delivered, Read, Answered), the approval card (Allow once, Always for this site, Deny, Details), Task and Quest card, nested task rows, the Quest strip, the Task drawer, the side sheet that holds the quest and the task, provider tiles, potions, hop / sparkles / poof, the streaming caret, the banner, the menu and the toast. Shared primitives live in `packages/ui` (shadcn/ui, restyled to these tokens).

## Behavior rules

- Durable server records drive every status; persuasive assistant text never does.
- Workbench labels are honest: Live or History for the Attempt shown, Unavailable (and why), Not used in this task, and Soon for the Browser tab. The Workbench opens on Summary, built from the hub's records with no model call. Computer control is shown as Later ([ADR 023](../decisions/023-workbench.md)).
- Approvals show the exact action, target, who asked (for example You → Pip → Tinker), why now, risk and expiry. Lifetimes: once, This Task (This Quest once a party joins), always for this site; high-risk actions offer once only and need a check box. Deny takes an optional reason. Expired requests cannot be approved late.
- A party member's request has no reply to sit in, so it shows where its Task does ([#698](https://github.com/otaviobarros/hobbots/issues/698)). While it is pending, the Task reads Needs you, and its row names the action: "Nettle · waiting on your approval: Delete files". The approval card shows on the lead's Task card in the chat, in the Quest tab under the tree, and at the top of the Task drawer. Deciding it there or in the Inbox decides the same request, and a card that was in view keeps its outcome line. A chat turn's own requests stay in their reply.
- Retire asks what happens to a hob's work (hand it off, let it finish, stop it now). Nothing is deleted; the hob moves to the Archive's Retired hobs.
- Archiving a thread never asks first and never deletes: a toast offers Undo, and Restore brings it back at any time. An archived thread leaves the hob panel, stays in search with an Archived tag, and opens read-only with no composer. Archive is disabled, with its reason, while the thread has a running or queued reply or a pending approval, so it never interrupts work. The hub's archived time drives the Archived state on every device.
- Closing the window keeps Hobbots in the menu bar by default; Settings › General lets the user change that and turn off Open at login. With it off, closing the last window while hobs work first says that their work will stop.
- A hob's reply is formatted from its Markdown: headings in Pixelify Sans and moonlight, square list markers, tables with 2px line borders and a panel header row, quotes on a panel fill (never a left-edge accent), inline code and code blocks in the monospace font on the deep ground. A code block names its language and has a Copy button; code is highlighted with the palette (keywords lantern, strings mint, numbers rose, names moonlight, types star, comments label). HTML in a reply shows as text. A link opens in the browser and shows its address beside its text. An image loads only after the reader presses "Show image from <host>", and only from an `https:` address. While a reply streams, a table appears only once its header is complete and a row only once it ends, so raw pipes never flash. Your own messages stay plain text, exactly as typed.
- Providers appear only when connected; keys typed by hand go to the OS keychain and never into chat.
- Connections that give a hob abilities beyond its runtime's own tools are called **Charms**: integrations with other apps and services (GitHub first, with Push; later Slack, Google Drive, any MCP server). They are connected once in Settings › Charms and granted per hob with a switch on its Charms tab; GitHub connects with the Hobbots GitHub App (recommended, it reaches only the repositories you install it on) or a sign-in already on the computer, and its tokens stay in the OS keychain on the hub. A hob can also get its own GitHub app from its Charms tab, so its work shows on GitHub under its own bot instead of your name. The hob profile's tabs are Profile, Charms and Permissions. Rules show Allow, Ask or Never; a rule Hobbots can only check, not enforce, says so.
- A hob running without the sandbox shows the Unsandboxed tag on its profile, chat header, Workbench header and Home tile. Turning the sandbox off asks for a confirmation check box.

## Implementation status

The validation slice ([ADR 009](../decisions/009-toolchain-and-validation-slice.md)) builds these screens in this style:

- **Home** is the landing page: the night scene with every hob and its live state (clicking a hob opens a peek under it with its state tag, role, what it is doing, Open chat and, while it works on a Task, Open Workbench; Escape or a click outside closes it), count chips (All, Need you, Working and Resting, each with its count; a hob with a waiting request counts as needs you) that filter the scene, dimming the hobs they leave out, Needs you (the first three waiting requests, oldest first, each opening in the Inbox, and how many more wait), Happening now, Recent threads, a Claude potion under AI plans, and Quests in progress (empty).
