# Design specification

Decided in [ADR 008](../decisions/008-design.md). This document records what the code must match. The [screen inventory](screens-and-flows.md) lists every screen and its states.

Hobbots is a modern AI chat app with a pixel-folklore character: small hobs that work at night in a lantern-lit workshop. Familiar interactions come first. The art, the hob animations and the names carry the personality; they never hide what a hob or an action actually does.

## Style tokens

The app has four themes, set with `data-theme` on the root element. Their values and rules are in [themes.md](themes.md) and [themes.json](themes.json). The app opens in Workshop Night, the night palette below, until a saved choice lands (#480). The table names today's uses; the redesign (#462) moves the screens onto the full set.

All text passes WCAG AA; the lowest pair is 5.6:1. Control borders are at least 3:1.

## Type

- **Atkinson Hyperlegible Next** for all reading text: chat, lists, buttons, fields. Body 15px; rows, menus, search fields and tabs may use the 14px UI style.
- No text goes below 12px anywhere, key hints and tags included.
- **Pixelify Sans** (ligatures off) only for headings, hob names and uppercase section labels, never below 14px.
- Fonts are bundled with the app; no network font loading.

## Shape and motion

- Pixel corners (a 4px clipped corner) on cards and buttons. Primary buttons have a 4px "press" edge and press down 2px.
- Focus is a 2px moonlight ring; inside clipped shapes it is drawn inset.
- Selection uses a fill (`#2c2a5a`), never a colored left edge. **No decorative left-edge accent borders** anywhere.
- Motion is stepped (pixel timing): hover a hob and it hops; working hobs bob; thinking shows blinking dots; streaming shows a moonlight caret; new messages, and the line that says how a reply ended (stopped, cut off, limited or failed), slide in; approve throws sparkles, deny shakes, forget poofs, sleep shows Zs. **Every animation stops with Reduce motion.** Nothing loops past 10 seconds or 3 cycles without new input; a state that needs to show uses a still frame.
- Each hob is a 14×14 pixel avatar generated from a seed (hat, gear, face, ears, beard, outfit, skin) plus one of six colors (Violet, Moss, Amber, Rose, Sky, Ember). The seed and color are saved with the hob, so the same hob looks the same on every device. The Hobbots mark, in the sidebar and the app icon, is the chief of the hobs: a 20×20 elder hob built from the same parts, in a gem crown with a lantern staff. In the sidebar the chief keeps still and its lantern speaks for the hobs: it looks around once when the app opens and on hover; while any hob works the lantern burns brighter with a slow flicker; while a request waits in the Inbox it raises a blazing amber lantern that pulses, shows a "!", links to the Inbox and says how many requests wait. With Reduce motion the colors, raised lantern and "!" stay and nothing moves. The needs-you mood follows the Inbox live: it shows as soon as a request arrives and ends once every request is decided; its link reads "Hobbots inbox, something needs you" or "Hobbots inbox, 3 things need you", counting stuck work too, and otherwise "Hobbots home". In a development build, `?chief=idle`, `?chief=working` or `?chief=needs-you` previews a mood. The sprite sheet defines nine states: idle, working, thinking, waiting, needs you, asleep, celebrating, failed, sending.

## Layout

- **Title strip**, 30px, with the window controls.
- **Sidebar**, on every screen after setup: 72px collapsed, 256px expanded, and never hidden. It starts collapsed. The chevron at the bottom and ⌘\ toggle it, and the device remembers the choice. Collapsed, it shows the chief of the hobs and an icon per section (Home, Inbox with its needs-you count, Usage, Settings), and hovering or focusing an icon names it after 300ms. Expanded, the labels fade in once the width settles. Under a divider come the hobs, each a 36px portrait tile with a still state mark (lantern when it needs you, mint while it works, none while it rests). A lead also carries the lantern mark while anything on its Quest needs you, a member's question or approval included, and an unread dot while its threads hold a message you have not read ([ADR 027](../decisions/027-quest-room.md)) and, expanded, its name and one state word: Needs you, Working, Paused or Resting. The hob whose page or chat is open lists its five most recent threads under its name while the sidebar is expanded. A hob's tile opens its card beside the sidebar: its portrait on a ground tinted with its hat color, its state tag, what it is doing, "On Aldric's quest" with its Task's state for each Quest it works on as a member (opening the Quest), its backpack (four items at most, never badged), and Open page and Profile. Its name also opens its page. Esc, a click outside, the close button or the tile closes it, and following any of its links closes it too. + Summon a hob comes last. From 1100px wide it pushes the page aside; narrower, it stays 72px in place and opens over the page with the sheet shadow. Icons are 1.6px line icons in Lamplight and 12×12 pixel art in Workshop, shown by the skin; inactive icons are `text-secondary`, and the current section gets the selected fill (`card` in Lamplight, `line` in Workshop).
- **Chat**: header with a back arrow to the hob's chats, then sprite and name (each opens the hob's page), thread title, role, a magnifier that opens Search this chat under the header (a count of the matching messages, then each match with who said it, when and the words marked in lantern; picking one scrolls the chat to it, the first Escape clears the words and the next closes it, and nothing found offers Search all of the hob's chats, which opens its Chats already searching for the same words), a ⋯ menu with Rename (the title becomes a field in the header: Enter saves, Escape keeps the old title), Pin to the top (a lantern pin beside the title) or Unpin, Search this chat, the hob's page, Export thread and Archive chat in rose (its toast's one action is Undo), brain chip (the runtime's tile, runtime · model · effort, and health), which in a thread opens the brain picker: each runtime with its models, or why it cannot think now; the efforts the current model takes, as a segmented row; "Also make it <Hob>'s default"; "Back to <Hob>'s default" when the thread has its own brain. Where a reply's brain differs from the reply before it, the transcript shows a centered note, "Now thinking with Codex · <model>", and after a switch before the next reply, "The next reply thinks with …". A limited or cut-off reply offers "Continue with <runtime>" for each other ready runtime beside Continue from here, Edit hob and a quest button for each running or pinned Quest, which opens its room. My messages are right-aligned under "You · 9:41", on a card-cornered bubble tinted with star whose bottom corner on my side is the tag corner (max 460px). A hob's reply starts with a row: its 30px tile in its hat color, its name in moonlight, a "Lead" tag tinted with its hat color, and the time; the reply sits under the name, past the tile. A new thread shows "A fresh page" with idea chips. Above a hob's reply, its steps sit in a panel box with a line border: ✓ done in mint, a blinking ● running in lantern, ◷ waiting for approval in lantern, ✗ failed and ⊘ not allowed in rose (with the reason under it, saying whether the hob's permissions, you or the sandbox refused it; a Hobbots tool call the hub refused reads "Hobbots refused this: …" under its ✗), ■ did not finish in label, and a bold ? in lantern for a tool call cut off while it ran, with "Cut off while it ran, so it may or may not have taken effect. Hobbots won't run it again. Check it before you continue." under it and, when opened, when it started. A command step's label reads what the command does: it leaves out leading `VAR=value` assignments that only set how output looks or switch a config file off, such as `GIT_CONFIG_GLOBAL=/dev/null` or `NO_COLOR=1`, and keeps any other, cuts a path of four parts or more to its last two (`…/src/app.ts`), and when the whole command is one Git command it leaves out `-C`, since the step names that folder. A push, which the step names by remote and branch, and a Git command with `--git-dir` or `--work-tree`, which is decided on more than one folder, keep every folder option. A tool step opens to show the tool, the action in the Permissions tab's words and what it acted on, then its Output on deep with a line border, in monospace, with secrets already redacted. Short output shows whole. Long output shows its first lines, a dashed line saying "148.9 kB left out here", and its last lines, which open scrolled to the end where an error usually is; each part scrolls past 240px. Under it, the link "Open the full log (165 kB)" opens the whole redacted log in the browser, or "The full log could not be kept." in label. A finished reply keeps its tool steps and drops its thinking. A step waiting for approval has its approval card under the steps, ringed in lantern: the hob's sprite and "<Hob> asks permission", when it expires, then what it wants to do as a plain question that names its files or what its command runs ("Delete scratch.txt?", "Run check.sh?"; other actions in the Permissions tab's words) with the exact command or path, what it acts on, who asked, why now and the risk; Allow once (approve) and Deny (deny). A high-risk request (a force push or a push to the default branch) shows "High risk · once only" in rose under Risk and a check box, "I checked this action. Let <Hob> do it this once.", which enables Allow once. Deny opens an optional reason with Deny and Cancel and Escape closes it. Once decided the ring turns mint with a pop for Allowed, rose with a shake for Denied with the reason, or label for Expired, and the buttons give way to that line. Composer: a plain-text input on deep with a control border, Send in lantern, Stop in rose, hint line. It takes no Markdown and shows no formatting toolbar: what is typed or pasted is sent as is, with every line break, tab, space and blank line, and pasted rich text keeps only its text. A draft of only whitespace is not sent. It grows with its text to 160px, then scrolls, and the transcript keeps its last message in view while it grows. Enter sends, Shift+Enter adds a line, and Enter that ends an IME composition never sends. The input clears as soon as a message is sent and keeps focus after sending and when a reply ends; a refused send puts its text back. Each thread and fresh page keeps its own unsent draft, also across a reload, and Up on an empty input brings back the last message sent in the thread to edit. The hint reads "Enter sends · Shift+Enter adds a line · ↑ brings back your last message". Stop ends only the current reply; Cancel lives on the Quest. Under a request that started a Task, a one-line **ticket** sits in the transcript ([ADR 027](../decisions/027-quest-room.md)): a card with a line edge and no ring (only the Needs-you tray is ringed in lantern), holding the party's sprites in a row, the quest flag, the title, "2 of 3 done" ("Aldric's task" for a Task alone, then "Aldric finished this", "Aldric could not finish this" or "Aldric stopped this" once it ends), a lantern "1 needs you" tag while something waits on you, a rose Stuck tag while work about it is stuck (below) and Open room. It updates live and never grows; a screen reader reads it as one control that names the Quest and its state. Several Quests in one thread each keep their own ticket. The chat itself gains only the moments that keep you informed: the lead's plan, a question that reached you ("Ada asks you", ringed in lantern, with your answer box, then "You answered: …" in mint; the same question is lit in Ada's lane), a quiet line when the lead answered a member for you, and the lead's finish. Under a message you send while a Quest runs, a **steer receipt** in `detail` with the hobs' 16px sprites follows it as the hub records each step: "Aldric is reading…", "Aldric got it", "Aldric got it → passing to Ada…" (each hob named when there are several), "Ada picked it up", or "Held: Ada is waiting on your approval"; it is announced politely, once per step. Stuck work is a blocker: work a hob was handed that ran out of tries, every turn ending early, or whose hob retired (IN-007). It shows on the ticket of the work it is about, as a rose Stuck tag, in the lane of the hob it was handed to, and in Inbox › Needs you, as a row that opens the same card beside the list. The card is ringed in rose: the hob's sprite and "<Hob> could not carry on", what the work was ("Reading the result of "Outline the launch""), a Stuck tag in rose, why ("It was tried 3 times, and every turn ended early." with how the last try ended, such as "You stopped the last try.", then "Details:" with the error that try recorded in monospace when it recorded one, or "Quill retired, so nobody can take it."), the next action in lantern ("Next: retry it" or "Next: dismiss it"), then Retry (primary when it is the next action, absent once the hob retired), Reassign (disabled, with "Reassign arrives once a Task can change owners."), Dismiss, and Open in the chat outside the chat. Retry starts the work again with as many tries as before, even where you had stopped it; Dismiss settles it; each choice is kept on the item and the card leaves.

Show technical steps in the room's header (in its ⋯ menu below 1024px; kept per device) shows the exact marks instead, in monospace (`↗` read, `✎` edit with "+12", `$` command with ✓ or its exit code in rose, `✕` deleted, `⊘` refused), and the Workbench Terminal always has every command. The now line says what the running step does in the same words ("Running the tests", "Looking through the files"). hand-off chips ("Landing copy → Ada", "← Landing copy from Quill"), your notes as dashed chips ("Your note: …", "← Your note, via Aldric"), a wait line ("Waits on Ada's preview link"), a live now line with blinking dots while a turn runs, and the result. The lane's ⋯ menu: Show in the Workbench, Retry (only when failed), Reassign… (a chip per hob the hub would accept and Keep it), Cancel task…; the lead's lane adds Pin quest and Cancel quest…. The menus work from the keyboard, and focus returns to their button when they close. Clicking a lane filters the Workbench to that hob; clicking it again shows everyone. ## Components

The component sheet defines: buttons (primary, approve, secondary, deny, quiet, disabled, icon, chip), switch (before the label that names it), segmented control, stepper, inputs, status tags, Workbench view labels, delivery steps (Sent, Delivered, Read, Answered), the approval card (Allow once, Always for this site, Deny, Details), the Quest ticket, the steer receipt, the Quest room header, the lane (beats, marks, hand-off and note chips, the lit question, the failed state, the attempt picker), the docked Workbench strip, the read-only xterm.js terminal, provider tiles, potions, hop / sparkles / poof, the streaming caret, the banner, the menu and the toast. In a segmented control the arrow keys, Home and End pick the segment they move to. A search field shows a `/` keycap while it is empty, `/` anywhere outside a text field focuses it, and Escape clears its text before it closes anything. Content that loads keeps its space: `Skeleton` is a still block (`text` for one line of a type style at 60% of its height, `block` for a tag, `tile` for a hob or provider tile, `bar` for a meter's empty track, `control` for a control that waits on its value, such as a segmented control, and `dot` for a switch or a round mark), filled with `line`, or `line-2` on a card, with tag corners and no shimmer or pulse in any theme. Its `WaitNote` shows nothing for the first second, then one polite status line in `detail` and `text-secondary` that names what the region waits on, and after 10 seconds, for a call outside the hub, a plain sentence and Try again as a quiet button. `useWaitStage` gives a region that clock (`none`, `slow`, `stuck`) from its first-load flag, so a refresh keeps the last value and shows no wait. Shared primitives live in `packages/ui` (shadcn/ui, restyled to these tokens).

## Behavior rules

- Durable server records drive every status; persuasive assistant text never does.
- A Quest tells you about itself by how much it matters ([ADR 027](../decisions/027-quest-room.md)). Needs you (a question that reached you, an approval, a failed Task) goes to the Inbox, marks the lead's tile and sends a desktop notification under "When a hob needs me". A message from the lead leaves an unread dot on the lead's tile, on the thread in its list and on the folded chat rail; the hub keeps what you have read, so the dot clears on every device. A finished Quest sends a desktop notification under "When work finishes". Routine progress notifies nobody. Clicking any of them opens the room with that lane lit.
- The Terminal is dark in every theme, with its own palette that passes contrast, as real terminals are.
- Workbench labels are honest: Live or History for the Attempt shown, Unavailable (and why), Not used in this task, and Soon for the Browser tab. Lanes and the Workbench are built from the hub's records with no model call ([ADR 027](../decisions/027-quest-room.md)). Computer control is shown as Later ([ADR 023](../decisions/023-workbench.md)).
- Approvals show the exact action, target, who asked (for example You → Pip → Tinker), why now, risk and expiry. Lifetimes: once, This Task (This Quest once a party joins), always for this site; high-risk actions offer once only and need a check box. Deny takes an optional reason. Expired requests cannot be approved late.
- A party member's request has no reply to sit in, so it shows where its Task does ([#698](https://github.com/otaviobarros/hobbots/issues/698)). While it is pending, the Task reads Needs you, and its row names the action: "Nettle · waiting on your approval: Delete files". The approval card shows in the Needs you tray and in the member's lane in the Quest room, the ticket reads "1 needs you" and the lead's tile carries the needs-you mark. Deciding it there or in the Inbox decides the same request, and a card that was in view keeps its outcome line. A chat turn's own requests stay in their reply.
- Retire asks what happens to a hob's work (hand it off, let it finish, stop it now). Nothing is deleted; the hob moves to the Archive's Retired hobs, where Bring back makes it active again with all its chats. Its rules and its own GitHub app stay gone.
- Archiving a thread never asks first and never deletes: a toast offers Undo, and Restore brings it back at any time. An archived thread leaves the hob's lists, stays in ⌘K search with an Archived tag, and opens read-only with no composer. Archive is disabled, with its reason, while the thread has a running or queued reply or a pending approval, so it never interrupts work. The hub's archived time drives the Archived state on every device.
- Closing the window keeps Hobbots in the menu bar by default; Settings › General lets the user change that and turn off Open at login. With it off, closing the last window while hobs work first says that their work will stop.
- A hob's reply is formatted from its Markdown: headings in Pixelify Sans and moonlight, square list markers, tables with 2px line borders and a panel header row, quotes on a panel fill (never a left-edge accent), inline code and code blocks in the monospace font on the deep ground. A code block names its language and has a Copy button; code is highlighted with the palette (keywords lantern, strings mint, numbers rose, names moonlight, types star, comments label). HTML in a reply shows as text. A link opens in the browser and shows its address beside its text. An image loads only after the reader presses "Show image from <host>", and only from an `https:` address. While a reply streams, a table appears only once its header is complete and a row only once it ends, so raw pipes never flash. Your own messages stay plain text, exactly as typed.
- Providers appear only when connected; keys typed by hand go to the OS keychain and never into chat.
- Connections that give a hob abilities beyond its runtime's own tools are called **Charms**: integrations with other apps and services (GitHub first, with Push; later Slack, Google Drive, any MCP server). They are connected once in Settings › Charms and granted per hob with a switch on its Charms tab; GitHub connects with the Hobbots GitHub App (recommended, it reaches only the repositories you install it on) or a sign-in already on the computer, and its tokens stay in the OS keychain on the hub. A hob can also get its own GitHub app from its Charms tab, so its work shows on GitHub under its own bot instead of your name. The hob profile's tabs are Profile, Charms and Permissions. Rules show Allow, Ask or Never; a rule Hobbots can only check, not enforce, says so.
- Reads from the hub draw their final frame at once. A page shows its header with what is already known, such as the hob's name and tile; a list (Your hobs, a hob's chats and History, the Inbox, the Archive, standing rules, the lanes and Workbench Files) shows three `Skeleton` rows in its own row shape; the chat transcript shows two reply blocks at `measure` width with the composer ready; the Inbox detail keeps its frame. Counts and summary lines are skeletons too, never 0 or "No chats yet" while asking. Hub reads add no wait line, because they are quick; after 10 seconds the region says the hub is slow and offers Try again, in case it is out of reach.
- A hob running without the sandbox shows the Unsandboxed tag on its profile, chat header, Workbench header and Home tile. Turning the sandbox off asks for a confirmation check box.
