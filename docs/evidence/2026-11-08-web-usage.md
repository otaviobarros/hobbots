# Web searches and pages read are recorded, counted and held to their limits

- **Date:** 2026-11-08
- **Issue:** [#1269](https://github.com/otaviobarros/hobbots/issues/1269)
- **Pull request:** PULL_REQUEST
- **Commit:** `COMMIT` on `claude/project-thread-vmsn61`
- **Setup:** Linux x86_64 container (Node 22), hub from source on SQLite and PostgreSQL 16, with the fake runtime and the local test site of [#1267](2026-11-08-web-fetch.md): a static resolver gave its names TEST-NET-3 addresses and only the fetcher's test-only `dialAs` mapped them to the loopback server. Claude Code's policy hooks ran in-process with the Agent SDK's hook inputs; no installed runtime, account or real site was used.
- **Result:** Passed with gaps. Every "Done when" case of #1269 holds at hub level and in the adapters' unit tests. Codex's next turn with search off is proven in the hub's rules, not on an installed Codex, and no screen shows the records yet ([#1271](https://github.com/otaviobarros/hobbots/issues/1271)).

## Gaps

- No screen shows the records, Settings › Web or a hob's own limits yet ([#1271](https://github.com/otaviobarros/hobbots/issues/1271)); they are reachable through the API.
- An installed Codex starting its next turn with search off after going over its per-turn limit, and a real Claude Code search refused by a limit, are not run ([#1272](https://github.com/otaviobarros/hobbots/issues/1272)).
- Tokens are estimated at one per four characters of the answer, not counted by the runtime's tokenizer.
