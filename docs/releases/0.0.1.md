# Hobbots 0.0.1 beta

The first public beta of Hobbots, for macOS on Apple silicon and Intel. It is an early build: expect rough edges, and back up your data before you rely on it. The [status page](../STATUS.md) lists what has been proven end to end and on which setup.

## Install

Download the `.dmg` for your Mac from the release and follow [Install the Mac app](../../README.md#install-the-mac-app). You need macOS 13 Ventura or later and at least one runtime, installed and signed in: [Claude Code](https://code.claude.com/docs/en/setup) 2.1.301 or later with your Claude plan, or [Codex](https://developers.openai.com/codex/cli) 0.166.0 or later with your ChatGPT plan. The app is not signed by an Apple developer yet, so macOS asks you to choose **Open Anyway** once.

## What is in it

- **Hobs on Claude Code and Codex.** Summon hobs on Claude, through Claude Code, or on ChatGPT, through Codex, each with its own name, model, instructions and folders. Hobs on either runtime can lead or join the same Quest.
- **Hobs that keep working.** Their threads stream, survive a crash or a restart, and keep running after you close the window.
- **Tools under your policy.** A hob uses tools inside its folders and the macOS sandbox. Each rule is Allow, Ask or Never. You answer an Ask once, for the Task, or always, from the chat or the Inbox, and History keeps every decision.
- **Tasks and Quests.** A hob splits a request into steps, brings in other hobs and leads them as a party. Questions go to the right lead, results reach hobs whose turn has ended, and nothing is lost when Hobbots restarts.
- **The Quest room and the Workbench.** Follow a Quest beside the lead's chat, a lane per hob. The Workbench shows each Task's summary, changed files and the commands it ran in a terminal.
- **GitHub through the Charm.** Connect GitHub once. Hobs you choose read issues and pull requests, push after your approval, and open pull requests and comments. A hob can also get its own GitHub App and work as its bot.
- **Settings, backup and diagnostics.** Settings shows whether everything is OK, makes a backup the Welcome screen can restore, and writes a redacted diagnostics report.
- **Remote access.** Allow your other devices on your Tailscale tailnet to use the hub, and revoke them at once.

## Known limits

- **Unsigned app.** Hobbots is not signed or notarized by Apple, so macOS blocks the first launch until you choose Open Anyway. A new version may ask again.
- **No automatic updates.** To update, quit Hobbots and replace the app with the one from the new `.dmg`. Your data stays in `~/Library/Application Support/Hobbots`.
- **Checked by hand on Apple silicon only.** Intel Macs run the release smoke tests in CI but have not been checked by hand.
- **Commit identity gaps.** A hob without its own GitHub App commits as you, so set `user.name` and `user.email` in your Git config first ([#741](https://github.com/otaviobarros/hobbots/issues/741)). A lead can still commit a member's work under its own identity ([#742](https://github.com/otaviobarros/hobbots/issues/742)).
- **Opening at login** has not been checked across a real log-out and log-in ([#49](https://github.com/otaviobarros/hobbots/issues/49)).
- **Browser use, hob memory and hobs managing other hobs** are not built yet.

## Report a problem

Open an [issue](https://github.com/otaviobarros/hobbots/issues/new/choose). Settings › Diagnostics makes a redacted report you can attach. Report security problems as [SECURITY.md](../../SECURITY.md) describes.
