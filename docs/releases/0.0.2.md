# Hobbots 0.0.2 beta

The second beta of Hobbots, for macOS on Apple silicon and Intel. Its headline is memory: hobs now remember what they learn between threads, and you can read, edit and undo all of it. It is still an early build, so back up your data before you rely on it. The [status page](../STATUS.md) lists what has been proven end to end and on which setup.

## Install or update

Download the `.dmg` for your Mac from the release and follow [Install the Mac app](../../README.md#install-the-mac-app). The requirements have not changed: macOS 13 Ventura or later and at least one runtime, installed and signed in: [Claude Code](https://code.claude.com/docs/en/setup) 2.1.301 or later with your Claude plan, or [Codex](https://developers.openai.com/codex/cli) 0.166.0 or later with your ChatGPT plan.

Coming from 0.0.1, quit Hobbots and drag the new app onto Applications, replacing the old copy. Your threads and settings stay in `~/Library/Application Support/Hobbots`. The app is still not signed by an Apple developer, so macOS may ask you to choose **Open Anyway** again.

## What is new

- **Hobs remember.** Hobbots now bundles the [ai-memory](https://github.com/akitaonrails/ai-memory) engine and runs it beside the hub, on your Mac only. Each hob has its own memory. It is briefed from it when a session starts, searches it and saves lessons to it with the new `memory_search` and `memory_save` tools, and what it does in a thread is captured and summed up there. Hobs on a Quest keep their own memory of the work they did for it.
- **The Memory page.** See what each hob has learned, edit or remove it, and write **About you**, which every hob reads. A hob's page shows its memory, and its chat shows when it saves something.
- **You choose what is shared.** A lesson a hob wants to share with a project or with every hob waits for you in the Inbox and in Memory › To review, where you share it as it is, edit it first or turn it down. Under the Trusting preset, project shares go through and show in Memory › What changed with Undo. A share with every hob always asks.
- **Memory runs on your own sign-ins.** Choose the memory model in Settings. It runs through Claude Code or Codex on the plan you already use, with no API key, or with no AI at all. Memory is in your backups and exports, and Diagnostics shows whether the engine is running.
- **Questions with choices.** A hob can ask you with two to four choices, one of them recommended, and you answer with a click or in your own words. Hobbots itself can ask you this way too.
- **Who hobs commit as.** When your Git config sets no name and email, Hobbots asks you once who hobs on your account commit as: your GitHub account at its noreply address, or a name and email you type. Hobs no longer get stuck asking each other ([#741](https://github.com/otaviobarros/hobbots/issues/741)).
- **A calmer composer.** While a hob is replying, the composer stays on one line with **Stop** and **Send after this reply**, and messages you send meanwhile wait their turn in order.
- **Start over.** Settings › Your data can delete everything the hub keeps and bring you back to the Welcome screen, with a backup offered in the same dialog.
- **Leaner Quests.** A lead that wakes up is told only what changed since its last turn, its rules once per session and each result once, instead of the whole Quest every time. Long Quests use far fewer tokens.
- **Usage counts the whole party.** Usage now includes what party members and steps spend on a Quest, and every run of a turn that a background command woke.

## Fixes

- A background command keeps running until it really ends, and a running lane names the command it is waiting on.
- A lead's routine progress stays out of its chat during a Quest.
- A note you leave carries on to the Task a lead delegates for it.
- Cancelling a Quest before its next step starts stops that step.
- The Quest room no longer scrolls: the Workbench's Terminal fits its dock, a finished Quest shows one fixed duration, and a running lane says when its hob last moved.
- Keyboard focus stays in place when you answer, retry or a backpack moves, and every screen stops scrolling past its content.
- A chat keeps its unread dot when the list arrives after the reply.
- A memory save made while memory was not answering is recorded as written once it lands, in the session that made it, and events sent late keep the time they happened.
- Backup restore and Start over refuse to touch a data folder that a running hub is using.

## Known limits

- **Unsigned app.** Hobbots is not signed or notarized by Apple, so macOS blocks the first launch until you choose Open Anyway, and a new version may ask again ([#945](https://github.com/otaviobarros/hobbots/issues/945)).
- **No automatic updates.** To update, replace the app with the one from the new `.dmg`.
- **Checked by hand on Apple silicon only.** Intel Macs run the release smoke tests in CI, but neither the app nor memory has been checked by hand on one.
- **Memory search is strict.** A search finds a page only when every word of the query is a whole word in it, so plain questions, word starts and hyphenated names can come back empty ([#1047](https://github.com/otaviobarros/hobbots/issues/1047)).
- **Long Codex commands.** A Codex command that runs longer than about 10 seconds can end its turn while still running, and then shows as uncertain ([#1048](https://github.com/otaviobarros/hobbots/issues/1048)).
- **Commit identity.** A lead can still commit a member's work under its own identity ([#742](https://github.com/otaviobarros/hobbots/issues/742)).
- **Browser use and hobs managing other hobs** are not built yet.
