# Hobbots 0.0.4 beta

The fourth beta of Hobbots, for macOS on Apple silicon and Intel and for Linux. Its headline is hobs that work as a team: they know who else is on the hub and what each one is good at, and a hob you trust can summon, change and retire other hobs while you approve each change and keep its history. Hobs can now also search the web and read pages, under per-site rules that keep your data from leaving through a page. It is still an early build, so back up your data before you rely on it. The [status page](../STATUS.md) lists what has been proven end to end and on which setup.

## Install or update

**Mac.** Download the `.dmg` for your Mac from the release and follow [Install the Mac app](../../README.md#install-the-mac-app). Coming from 0.0.3, quit Hobbots and drag the new app onto Applications, replacing the old copy. Your threads, memory, skills and settings stay in `~/Library/Application Support/Hobbots`, and the hub backs them up before it updates its database. The app is still not signed by an Apple developer, so macOS may ask you to choose **Open Anyway** again.

**Linux.** Download the package for your system and follow [Install on Linux](../../README.md#install-on-linux), then install it over the old one.

The requirements have not changed: at least one runtime, installed and signed in: [Claude Code](https://code.claude.com/docs/en/setup) 2.1.301 or later with your Claude plan, or [Codex](https://developers.openai.com/codex/cli) 0.166.0 or later with your ChatGPT plan. On macOS, version 13 Ventura or later.

## What is new

- **Hobs see each other.** Every hob gets the hub's roster: each hob's name, role and what it is good at, and which Charms it holds. A hob reads another hob's card with `read_hob`, so it can tell you who can push to GitHub or hand work to the right hob. You edit what a hob is good at on its profile, and you can hide a hob from the others. No path through the roster reaches a hob's instructions, memory, threads or Tasks.
- **The Summoning Charm.** Grant it to a hob from its Charms tab, or to every hob from Settings, and that hob can summon, change, pause, wake and retire other hobs, and itself if you allow it. Each field (name, role, brain, folders, Charms, retire and more) is Allow, Ask or Never, and a hob without the Charm hands the work to one that has it.
- **Summon and change cards.** A summon asks as one card with a row per new hob, where you Edit, Allow or Deny each row or all of them, and you apply what is created. A change to several hobs gets a before and after per hob. **Always allow** sets that field to Allow on the manager's Charm, as a change made by you.
- **No escalation.** A summoned or changed hob gets at most what its manager has. A hob can never widen permissions, turn the sandbox off, touch credentials, decide approvals or grant the Summoning Charm, to anyone or to itself.
- **Caps for summons that don't ask.** Summons that run without asking you stop at 4 live per manager and 3 per Task tree, both configurable, after which the next one asks. Summons you approve, and the number of hobs on a hub, are never capped.
- **History, Revert and the Changes log.** A hob's profile has a **History** tab with every change, who made it and a diff, and **Revert** for the latest one. Settings › Changes lists changes across every hob, and the profile says which hob summoned it.
- **Retire a hob with open work.** **Stop and hand over** gives its open Tasks to the hob that asked, and **Finish, then retire** lets it finish them first and take no new work.
- **Search the web.** A new built-in operation turns on each runtime's own search: Claude Code's `WebSearch`, and Codex held to cached search from OpenAI's index. Set it to Allow or Never per hob.
- **Read pages.** Hobs read pages with `web_fetch`, which the hub runs under **Use the web**: GET only, no cookies or sign-ins, the page turned into readable text, and the first page on a new site asking you under Careful. Your computer, your local network and cloud metadata addresses stay out of reach, and every redirect is decided again. A reply that used the web lists its sources, and **Open** on a page step shows the text the hob got.
- **Your data doesn't leave through a page.** Once a hob has searched or read a page in a session, anything that could send your data elsewhere asks you: a URL it wrote itself, a new host a command reaches, a Charm write, a push or a memory share. Opening a link it found follows the normal site rules.
- **Web usage and limits.** Searches and pages read are recorded per turn and per hob, with limits per turn and per day in Settings › Web, where you can also block sites. [PRIVACY.md](../../PRIVACY.md) is new and says what leaves your computer, and where it goes.
- **Skill updates.** A skill imported from Git is checked for updates once a day and on **Check now**. An update shows its diff and flags what deserves a careful look, such as a new script or hidden text. **Update automatically** applies clean updates and holds flagged ones for you.
- **A repository's own skills.** When a hob works in a repository with `.claude/skills` or `.agents/skills`, Hobbots offers them once for that project and imports them from its default branch, so a branch can't change what a running hob loads.
- **Try a skill in a chat, and skill health checks.** **Try in a chat** opens a chat where only that draft is loaded. The Skills Overview flags skills nobody uses, hobs behind on a revision, name clashes and vague descriptions, and shows each hob's skill load.
- **Setting up a hob that writes code.** Instead of asking who hobs commit as, Hobbots asks "Will this hob write code?" and points you to set up GitHub. You can also link a GitHub App you already have to a hob from its Charms tab.

## Fixes

- A lead no longer decides an approval that its member's command asked you for ([#1216](https://github.com/otaviobarros/hobbots/issues/1216)).
- Hobs no longer set their own commit identity on their first commit, so it goes through with no refused step ([#1213](https://github.com/otaviobarros/hobbots/issues/1213)).
- After **Stop waiting**, a hob no longer starts the same command again in the background ([#1217](https://github.com/otaviobarros/hobbots/issues/1217)).
- Workbench Changes shows the work of an Attempt that was killed, and Quest members show paths from the same folder ([#1218](https://github.com/otaviobarros/hobbots/issues/1218), [#643](https://github.com/otaviobarros/hobbots/issues/643)).
- A Codex hob can write to an approved folder under `~/Documents`, and macOS is asked for access when you add one. Its `python3` calls no longer print an Xcode cache error ([#1225](https://github.com/otaviobarros/hobbots/issues/1225)).
- Leads pass your answers on instead of replacing them, credit milestones to the right hob, and their summaries match what the hub accepted ([#1219](https://github.com/otaviobarros/hobbots/issues/1219)).
- Questions the hub asks you itself can now be translated ([#1045](https://github.com/otaviobarros/hobbots/issues/1045)).
- Codex apps, plugins, browser and computer use tools stay off for hobs, as the Codex adapter always meant.
- Memory names a chat page by its title instead of its session ID, and a page's History names who wrote each version ([#1316](https://github.com/otaviobarros/hobbots/issues/1316), [#1317](https://github.com/otaviobarros/hobbots/issues/1317)).
- Small Skills, Start over and setup fixes from the 0.0.3 smoke test: rollback wording, the skills folder you set, the backup folder Start over uses, and setup showing the effort it picks ([#1220](https://github.com/otaviobarros/hobbots/issues/1220)).

## Known limits

- **Unsigned app.** Hobbots is not signed or notarized by Apple, so macOS blocks the first launch until you choose Open Anyway, and a new version may ask again ([#945](https://github.com/otaviobarros/hobbots/issues/945)).
- **No automatic updates.** To update, replace the app with the one from the new `.dmg`, or install the new Linux package over the old one.
- **Linux and Intel Macs are checked in CI only.** Only macOS on Apple silicon has been checked by hand with real hobs.
- **Codex search.** Codex is offered search only where Use the web answers Allow, no site is blocked and pages are allowed each turn, so under Careful a Codex hob reads pages with `web_fetch` and does not search. Search is also offered only on Codex releases the contract tests checked, 0.166.0 and 0.167.0. A run with real models found cases where Never for Search the web did not hold on Codex; Hobbots has since changed when it offers Codex's search, which is not yet checked with real models. [PRIVACY.md](../../PRIVACY.md) has the details.
- **Web page limit on Claude Code.** Two pages read in parallel can both pass a one-page-a-turn limit ([#1335](https://github.com/otaviobarros/hobbots/issues/1335)).
- **Codex approvals.** A pending approval can expire when another chat with the same Codex hob finishes ([#1334](https://github.com/otaviobarros/hobbots/issues/1334)).
- **Summoning.** A hob in a plain chat can't ask you a question, so it may ask in its reply or pick on its own ([#1330](https://github.com/otaviobarros/hobbots/issues/1330)). Editing a summon row can switch that hob to Claude Code at medium effort ([#1331](https://github.com/otaviobarros/hobbots/issues/1331)), and a decided row shows the raw tool result ([#1325](https://github.com/otaviobarros/hobbots/issues/1325)).
- The v0.0.4 smoke test filed more small findings, [#1325](https://github.com/otaviobarros/hobbots/issues/1325) to [#1341](https://github.com/otaviobarros/hobbots/issues/1341), and its [evidence record](../evidence/2026-11-09-v0-0-4-mac-smoke.md) lists what was not run.
- **Browser use** is not built yet ([#382](https://github.com/otaviobarros/hobbots/issues/382)): the Workbench's Browser tab still says Coming soon.
